Thank you for contributing to Vetro. This agreement explains the rights you
give us when you send a contribution, so that the project can keep offering
the community edition as open source and, at the same time, a commercial
edition and commercial licences that fund its development.

It is an agreement between you ("You") and Lucian Medrihan, the maintainer of
Vetro [to check: or the company that will own the project], and his
successors and assigns ("We", "Us").

## 1. Definitions

- **Contribution**: any work of authorship (code, documentation, images,
  tests, configuration) that You submit to Us for inclusion in Vetro, by pull
  request, patch, issue, e-mail or any other means, except what You clearly
  mark as "Not a Contribution" when You send it.
- **Vetro**: the software projects We maintain under that name, in any
  edition (public or private).

## 2. What stays yours

You keep the copyright in Your Contributions. This agreement gives Us a
licence; it does not transfer ownership. You may use Your Contributions in any
other way You like.

## 3. Copyright licence

You grant Us and the recipients of software We distribute a perpetual,
worldwide, non-exclusive, free of charge, royalty-free, irrevocable licence to
reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform,
distribute and sublicense Your Contributions and such derivative works, **under
any licence terms, including proprietary and commercial ones**.

## 4. Patent licence

You grant Us and the recipients of software We distribute a perpetual,
worldwide, non-exclusive, free of charge, royalty-free, irrevocable patent
licence to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import and otherwise
transfer Your Contribution, where the licence applies only to the patent
claims You can license that are necessarily infringed by Your Contribution
alone or by its combination with Vetro. If anyone starts patent litigation
claiming that Vetro or a Contribution infringes a patent, the patent licences
granted to that person under this agreement end on the date the litigation is
filed.

## 5. Our promise to the community

For every Contribution We accept into the public repository, We will keep it
available under the open source licence of that repository (at the time of
this draft, proposed: AGPL-3.0-only, ADR 0034), or under another licence
approved by the Open Source Initiative. The licence in section 3 lets Us also
use it in commercial editions; it never lets Us withdraw the open source
version.

## 6. Your statements

You confirm that:
1. each Contribution is Your original work, or You have the right to submit
   it under this agreement (for third-party material, You tell Us its origin
   and licence when You submit it, and We decide whether to accept it);
2. if Your employer or a client has rights in what You create (for example
   under an employment contract), You have received permission to make the
   Contribution on its behalf, or it has waived those rights;
3. to Your knowledge, the Contribution does not infringe anyone's rights and
   does not contain material You are not allowed to share (confidential code,
   leaked sources, keys, other people's personal data).

You will tell Us if any of these statements stops being true.

## 7. Moral rights

To the extent the law allows, You agree not to assert moral rights in Your
Contributions against Us or the recipients of Vetro in a way that would
prevent the uses permitted by this agreement. Where moral rights cannot be
waived (for example the right to be named as the author under Italian law),
this section does not affect them. [to check]

## 8. No obligation, no warranty

We are not obliged to use any Contribution. You provide Your Contributions
"as is", without warranties or conditions of any kind, and You are not
expected to provide support for them, unless You choose to.

## 9. Law

This agreement is governed by Italian law [to check: or the law of the
future company's seat]. It is the entire agreement about Contributions and
applies to every Contribution You submit after accepting it.

## How to accept

Open a pull request and, when asked by the CLA check, write in a comment:

> I have read the Vetro Individual Contributor Licence Agreement and I accept it.

The check records Your GitHub account, the date and the version of this text.
Contributions on behalf of a company need a corporate agreement: write to the
