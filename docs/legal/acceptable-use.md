# Vetro Acceptable Use Policy

> **Draft** (product track B1). Not in force. To be reviewed by a lawyer
> together with the terms of service of `vetro.lol` (phase B4). Points marked
> [to check] need a decision.

Vetro runs a complete Android-compatible system in your browser and lets you
see what apps do, from the outside. That power is meant for understanding,
testing and protecting software and people. This policy says what Vetro is
for and what it must not be used for.

## Where this policy applies

- **Binding** for the hosted service (`vetro.lol`, `app.vetro.lol` and the
  API), for accounts, the Pro and Team plans, the Pro module and any
  commercial licence: breaking it is a breach of the terms of service, and We
  may suspend or close the account.
- **For the open source community edition**, the software licence alone
  decides what you may do with the code; this policy does not add
  restrictions to it. It states what the project stands for, and what We
  expect from anyone who reports issues, contributes or asks for help.

## What Vetro is for

- **Security research**: analysing how apps behave, looking for
  vulnerabilities, studying malware in an isolated environment, with
  responsible disclosure of what you find (tell the vendor first, give them
  reasonable time).
- **Privacy auditing**: finding out which data an app collects and where it
  sends it, for yourself, as a journalist or researcher, for a client that
  commissioned the audit, or for a regulator or court.
- **Developers testing their own apps**: debugging, checking network
  traffic, reproducing bugs with record and replay, testing on a clean
  device.
- **Education and interoperability**: learning how Android and ARM64 work,
  teaching, building compatible software, as far as the law allows.

## What you must not do

1. **Anything unlawful** in your country or in Italy, where the service is
   operated [to check], including breaking computer crime, data protection,
   copyright, consumer protection or export control laws.
2. **Access systems or accounts without authorisation**: no credential
   stuffing, account takeover, using others' sessions or tokens, or testing
   servers you do not own or have no written permission to test. Observing an
   app inside Vetro does not authorise attacks on its servers.
3. **Process other people's personal data without a legal basis**: no
   surveillance or tracking of individuals, no stalkerware, no harvesting of
   personal data from apps or services. Audits of apps are fine; spying on
   the people who use them is not.
4. **Fraud and platform abuse**: fake installs, ad or click fraud, fake
   reviews, account farms, bots that evade a service's limits at scale, or
   bypassing an app's payment or licence checks to use it without paying.
5. **Piracy**: distributing apps, games or content you have no right to
   distribute, or removing their protection to share them.
6. **Harmful software against others**: developing, testing or spreading
   malware, spyware or exploits intended for use against people or systems
   without their consent. Analysing malware to defend against it is
   welcome.
7. **Abusing the hosted service**: attacking or overloading it or other
   users, mining cryptocurrency, using the network relay to hide the origin
   of attacks or spam, sharing account access beyond your plan, or trying to
   defeat the licence checks of the Pro module.
8. **Misleading use of the name**: presenting a modified Vetro, or a service
   built on it, as the official Vetro, or presenting Vetro as a Google or
   Android product.

## Your responsibility

You are responsible for having the rights and permissions for the apps you
install, the data you analyse and the systems you contact. When in doubt
(for example about an app's terms or a research exemption in your country),
ask a lawyer before you start. Vetro's analysis stays in your browser: We do
not see your sessions unless you save or share them, but We may act on
reports of abuse.

## Reporting

Report misuse of Vetro, or a security problem in Vetro itself, to the
maintainer [to check: `abuse@vetro.lol` and `security@vetro.lol` once mail
exists]. Please do not open public issues for security problems.

We may update this policy; the date of the current version will be shown
here, and significant changes will be announced to account holders.
